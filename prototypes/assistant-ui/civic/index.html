    How answers work

    1. It reads only HEAL-AI’s documents

      Reports, playbooks, templates and resources we have published. Nothing else.

    2. It answers in plain words, then shows its sources

      Numbers like [1] take you to the document each point comes from.

    3. It says when it cannot answer

      If the material does not cover a question, or it is about someone’s care, it tells you and points you to people who can help.

    Inspired by

    This direction borrows from public-sector design systems known for plain language and accessibility.

    • GOV.UK Design System (opens in a new tab)

      design-system.service.gov.uk

      Borrowed: the warning-text pattern, the phase banner with a Beta tag, the yellow-and-black focus state and the plain-English writing style.

    • NIST AI Risk Management Framework (opens in a new tab)

      nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework

      Borrowed: sober, trustworthy-AI framing, quick-link question buttons and clear, labelled section headings.

    • U.S. Web Design System (opens in a new tab)

      designsystem.digital.gov

      Borrowed: alert boxes with a heavy left border for errors, and its accessible-by-default component rules.

    Patterns only — no logos, brand names in the UI, copy or paid fonts were taken.
