Our Process

A structured, four-step evaluation grounded in Stanford's FURM framework.

Every AI tool we evaluate moves through the same four phases. The output is a written report shared with clinical and operational leadership — and now with you.

FAIR USEFUL RELIABLE

The framework

FURM: Fair, Useful, and Reliable Models.

FURM is Stanford's peer-reviewed framework for evaluating clinical AI. Every report we publish — and every template in this toolkit — maps back to its three axes. Tools must demonstrate they are fair across populations, useful at the point of care, and reliable in production.

Source: Stanford Center for Biomedical Informatics Research · Open-access

    Patient Partner Panel

    Patient voices, by design — at Stanford, and at your institution.

    The Patient Partner Panel is the structural mechanism through which patient perspectives shape AI evaluations. Below: how we run ours, and how you can build one.

    Stanford's implementation

    How the Stanford panel works.

    Ten volunteer patient partners — recruited from Stanford Health Care's diverse patient community — meet routinely to review AI tools under evaluation. Each partner completes fundamentals training in clinical AI, ethics, and facilitation before reviewing their first tool.

    For your institution

    Building a panel at your health system.

    Use this guide layer as a practical scaffold. Each section reflects what we wish we'd known starting out — recruitment hurdles, training depth, facilitation norms, compensation models, and how to right-size the program for a smaller institution.

    Adapting the Process / Playbook

    Take this process and make it yours.

    Stanford's process was built for Stanford. The Playbook helps you adapt it — governance structure, recruitment, IT integration, compensation models, and scaling — for the realities of your institution.

    DiME Playbook

    The cross-institutional reference for adopting HEAL-AI's process.

    Published in partnership with the Digital Medicine Society (DiME), the Playbook is a step-by-step guide for health systems implementing structured AI evaluation — from a one-person ethics consult to a multi-departmental governance program.
